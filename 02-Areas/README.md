# 02-Areas

**Definition:** Ongoing responsibilities with a standard to maintain.

**Characteristics:**
- No specific end date - continuous improvement
- Have standards/criteria that define "done"
- Examples: Health, Finance, Learning, Career

**Current Areas:**
- [Hermes Tools](02-Areas/) - Omniroute launcher and automation tools
- Task Management
- Learning & Development

**Organization:**
- Each area gets its own folder
- Focus on maintenance and improvement
- Link to related projects/resources

## Area Guidelines

Areas are ongoing responsibilities without a specific end date, such as:
- Health & Fitness
- Finances
- Career Development
- Home Maintenance
- Relationships
- Personal Growth

Unlike projects, areas never get "completed" - they require continuous attention.

---

## Notes for Next AI/Human

- Areas are different from projects (no end date)
- Each area should have regular review cycles
- Tasks from areas may spawn new projects
- Keep area-related files organized by topic
