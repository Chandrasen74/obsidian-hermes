## HOW REWARDS WORK THIS SEASON 🎉 

Season 7 was our 5th Mech Arena Anniversary Season, and we wanted everyone who showed up and played to walk away with something. So on top of the usual win rewards, every player on an active team receives two bonuses this time:

- **100 A-Coins participation bonus** - paid to every player, regardless of results.
- **20 A-Coins per match played** - paid to every player for each match with a recorded result.

Your win rewards are calculated the same way as always: wins × the per-win amount for your bracket.

*This was an anniversary thing. Don't count on it coming back next season.*
