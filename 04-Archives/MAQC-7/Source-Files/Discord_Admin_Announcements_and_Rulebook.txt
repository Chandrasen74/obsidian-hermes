# 🚨 MAQC SEASON 7 BEGINS! 🎉🔥

Pilots, we are absolutely thrilled to announce the commencement of **Mech Arena Quarterly Clash (MAQC) Season 7**!

This isn't just any regular season. Season 7 coincides with the monumental **5th Anniversary of Mech Arena**! To celebrate this massive milestone, Plarium has generously stepped up with a truly *ludicrous* prize pool for our competitors.

Are you sitting down? Because this season, you are fighting for a record-shattering:

## 💰 **5,000,000 A-COINS!** 🏆

This is officially the biggest, most rewarding season in the history of the competition.

---

## 🗓️ REGISTRATION PERIOD

Registrations are **LIVE NOW!**

* **Opens:** TODAY (3rd August)
* **Closes:** 15th August, 6:00 AM UTC

Gather your squad, get your mechs in order, and lock in your registration before the deadline.

---

## 🚀 WHAT IS NEW IN SEASON 7?

To make this anniversary season our most competitive and balanced yet, we are introducing some major upgrades to the MAQC format:

* 📆 **6 Weeks of Play:** The clash has been extended! Prepare for a grueling and glorious 6-week campaign to claim the top prizes.
* ⚔️ **Best of 3 (Bo3) Match Format:** We are waving goodbye to single-match battles. Matches will now be played in a Bo3 format to ensure victories rely strictly on skill and strategy, and not just luck in a single drop.
* 📊 **10 Matchmaking Brackets:** We are expanding to 10 dynamic brackets. This means an even more granular distribution of teams based on SP, guaranteeing the fairest and tightest matchups we've ever had.
* ⚖️ **Reworked Dispute System:** We have completely overhauled our internal system for handling and resolving disputes to ensure faster, clearer, and fairer rulings for all teams.

---

Thank you for your patience, and keep those registrations coming! 🚀# 🏆 MECH ARENA QUARTERLY CLASH (MAQC) SEASON 7 – OFFICIAL RULEBOOK 
All participants are required to read and acknowledge this rulebook in full before registering.

## 📅 SECTION 1: TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE & KEY TIMELINES

### 🔹 Tournament Overview
* **Format:** Best of 3 battles.
* **Team Size:** Minimum 3 players, maximum 5 players.
* **Substitutes:** No substitutes allowed.
* **Maps:** Different maps will be designated each week.
* **Authority:** Admins’ rulings are final and non-negotiable.

### 🔹 Registration Windows & Deadlines
* **Registrations Open:** Today
* **Initial Registration Period:** From 3rd August to 15th August , 6:00 AM UTC
* **Extended Registration Period:** From 17th August to 23rd August, 6:00 AM UTC *(Strict cutoff. No further registrations accepted after this time).*
* **Week-1 Duration:** From 17th August to 23rd August, 6:00 AM UTC

⚠️ **CRITICAL NOTE:** Registering after the Initial Registration Deadline (August 15th) will completely void your Week-1 results.

---------## 📝 SECTION 2: REGISTRATION RULES & ROSTER POLICIES

### 🔹 Registration Rules
1. **Official Channel:** Team leaders must register using the official MAQC website.
2. **Single Submission:** Do not submit the registration form more than once. Multiple submissions by the same team will result in **immediate disqualification without warning**.
3. **One Mech Arena Account Per Team:** A leader can participate with multiple teams as long as his Mech Arena ID is different. If a player is found registered on multiple teams, **all teams involved will be disqualified**.

### 🔹 Roster Changes & Team Status
* **No-Penalty Changes:** Permitted only before the Initial Registration closes (August 15th).
* **Penalized Changes:** Permitted only before the Extended Registration closes (August 23rd).
* **Unregistered Players:** Having an unregistered player in a match will result in immediate, non-negotiable removal from the tournament.

### 🔹 Teams Removed from Week-1 Matchmaking
If a team does not appear on the Week-1 matchmaking list, they were removed due to:
* Incorrect or incomplete form submission.
* Duplicate team submissions.
* Players registered on multiple teams.
* Team captain not being present on the Discord server.

### 🔹 Information Updates
After the deadline of the match week, team leaders will be allowed one opportunity to update their registered SP to better align with their current status (which will be announced every Sunday of the match weeks). This allows members to participate in MAQC without inhibiting their in-game progression. Note that team leaders may only increase their SP, but not reduce it, so ensure that no errors are made during the updates. Any changes made to the registered SP may or may not affect the matchmaking system.

👉 **Captain Requirement:** While all team members are not required to be in the Discord server, **team captains must be present** to receive announcements, coordinate matches, and handle disputes.

---------## 🔄 SECTION 3: RE-REGISTRATION & MATCH INSTRUCTIONS

### 🔹 Re-Registration Policy
* Teams missing from Week-1 due to registration errors may re-register before the Extended Registration Deadline (August 23rd, 6:00 AM UTC).
* **Penalty:** Re-registering teams will receive an **automatic loss (auto-loss) for Week-1**.
* No additional correction opportunities will be provided after this period.
* **Player Removal:** To remove a player from the team (with no replacement), create a ticket in <#1533561855954653356>  and provide an appropriate reasoning before the match week deadline.
* Teams removed after the first week will no longer be able to re-register.

### 🔹 Match Gameplay Rules
* **Format:** Best of 3 battles format played on the designated maps of the week. Each team will play 3 CPC battles on the designated map of the week. First team to win 2 battles shall be the winner of the match.
* **Rematches:** No rematches allowed unless **both teams mutually agree**.
* **Player Verification:** No unregistered players or unregistered IDs are allowed in the match.
* **Absences:** If a player is absent, they may be substituted with an in-game bot. Need both leaders to agree. 
* **Lobby Settings:** Match lobby bot option is set to  **“Equalize”**.  
* **Disconnections/Crashes:** Match will **not** stop for disconnections or crashes. For critical issues, affected players must:
  1. Take a screenshot immediately.
  2. Report it to tournament moderators by creating a ticket in <#1533561855954653356>.
  3. Provide proof.
  *Event moderators will determine the appropriate course of action.*

---------## ⏰ SECTION 4: MATCH SCHEDULING & CONTACT POLICY

### 🔹 Match Window
* Matches must be played between **Monday → Sunday (6:00 AM UTC cutoff)** of the match week.
* Teams are expected to contact their opponents as early as possible to schedule. Opponent Discord IDs can be found in the match list.

### 🔹 No-Response Protocol
Ping your opponents in your battle post of the week as soon as possible. If your opponent does not respond after **72 hours** from the release of the matchmaking list:
1. Create ticket in <#1533561855954653356> and explain your situation.
2. You may be awarded an auto-win.
3. Notify mods about your afk opponent before Friday 6am UTC, after that we won't accept any request.
3. In clear cases of non-response, the unresponsive team may be completely removed from the tournament.
*Note: You must have made a reasonable, clear attempt at communication for your case to be considered.*

⚠️ **Mutual Unresponsiveness:** If neither team raises a dispute before the weekly deadline, **both teams will be marked as unresponsive** and both may be disqualified from subsequent weeks.

### 🔹 Matchmaking Difficulties & Fixed Times
* If scheduling is impossible, leaders must create a ticket in <#1533561855954653356> **before 6:00 AM UTC on Friday** of the match week.
* Moderators will assign a **fixed match time** only if contacted before this Friday deadline. Any request after Friday 6am UTC will not be considered.
* The match *must* be played at that assigned time. 
* Once match time is set either by both leaders or by mods, Leader who's making the custom match will share the match ID in their battle post of the week. 
* At least one representative from either team must show up. Failure to show up will result in an immediate forfeit for the no-show team(s).
* **Either team is obliged to only wait for 10 minutes post the agreed upon time for the opponents to join in**; if the opponents don't join in, ping MAQC-MOD in your battle post of the week.

---------## 🚫 SECTION 5: PLAYER CONDUCT & BRACKET VIOLATIONS

### 🔹 Player Conduct Expectations
All players must adhere to server rules when communicating and scheduling. This strictly applies to private DMs and in-game chats.
* **Prohibited Conduct:** Verbal abuse, inappropriate content, and deliberate instigation.
* **Reporting:** Report misconduct to the moderation team with sufficient proof by creating a ticket in <#1533561855954653356>. 
* **Punishments:** Misconduct can result in warnings, team disqualification, or blacklisting from future MAQC seasons.

---------## 📊 SECTION 6: SQUAD POWER (SP) DEFINITION & VIOLATION MANAGEMENT

### 🔹 Definition of "Max SP"
"Max SP" refers strictly to the **highest possible Squad Power achievable** using fully equipped mechs, weapons, pilots, implants, and mods.

❌ **This does NOT include:**
* Reduced SP builds
* Preferred setups
* Unequipped mods or implants
* Artificially lowered hangars

*Note: All teams must use standard 5-mech hangars for SP calculation. Intentional SP manipulation will result in immediate disqualification.*


### 🔹 Bracket & Squad Power (SP) Violations
All teams are expected to have registered at the **absolute maximum SP achievable** by the players in the team. 

* **Tolerance**
   A tolerance will be applied to the SP brackets to avoid being too restrictive, thus allowing for more flexibility when creating matchups. There are two Tolerances that one should be aware of:
1. 10% SP Tolerance - Current SP must not exceed 10% above the registered SP
2. 5% SP Bracket Tolerance - Current SP must not exceed 5% above the Bracket SP
Teams are required to satisfy both tolerances, and failure to do so will result in penalties.
* **Reporting an Over-Limit Opponent:**
  1. Create a ticket in <#1533561855954653356>.
  2. Provide clear screenshots and sufficient proof for investigation.
  3. **Do not engage** with the suspects; await the final decision.
* **Penalties:** Those found to have exceeded their SP Bracket will be disqualified from the match week and moved up to an appropriate bracket.
* **No appeals** will be accepted against this ruling.

* Provide **clear, unedited screenshots**.
* ⚠️ *Warning: False accusations made maliciously may result in penalties against the accuser.*

* **Honour System, Penalties & Compensation**
MAQC Season 7 introduces the Honour System to encourage fair play amongst participants. It is designed to punish rule-breaking, compensate victims, and not disrupt the flow of the event.

For any serious infraction of the rules, a Moderator may apply a demerit to the offending party. The effects of a demerit are as follows:

1. Reduces the final reward of the offending team by 25%
2. Increases the final reward of the affected team by 10%
3. Penalties can be stacked
---------## 🎁 SECTION 7: REWARDS, COMPLIANCE & LEGALITIES

### 🔹 Total Prize Pool
* **A-Coins:** 5,000,000 A-Coins.

### 🔹 Reward Distribution System 
* The reward system splits and distributes the     prize pool amongst the teams based off their performance during MAQC. The rewards of a given team are calculated based off the registered SP, number of wins, and any penalties/compensation (if applicable). This means that a team with zero wins may receive no rewards.

### 🔹 Reward Claims
* Rewards will be distributed several days after the tournament concludes.
* A deadline for missing reward reports will be announced. Claims raised after the deadline will not be processed.

### 🔹 Conduct & Compliance
All players must comply with the **Plarium Terms of Service** and **Discord Terms of Service**. Immediate removal will trigger for:
* Matchmaking manipulation / Boosting
* SP abuse / Cheating / Fake evidence
* Harassment or misconduct
