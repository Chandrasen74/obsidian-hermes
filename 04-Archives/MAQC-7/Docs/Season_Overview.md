## Key rulings and precedents

1. Bot match procedure (W5 Q&A, mod night fury): must play full Bo3 vs in-game bots; Equalize setting; minimum 1 human pilot; no locked time needed with no opponent; screenshots per game in battle post.

2. Roster removal (Ticket #583, night fury, 20 Sep): removal allowed anytime, no penalty, team cannot drop below 2, SP recalculated if highest SP player removed. Remove before the week's MM list for even bracket pairing.

3. Scheduling lock rule (W2 #671 precedent, night fury): a valid lock requires ONE exact UTC time plus an explicit "yes" from BOTH leaders in the battle post. Giving a range and having the opponent pick part of it does not count. Cost: auto-loss for both teams.

4. Gross misconduct / DM threats (Ticket #267, night fury, 24 Sep): sending violent threats, abusive profanity, and physical confrontation challenges in DMs is a Code of Conduct violation resulting in immediate season disqualification of the offending team. Victim team receives a bot match on the scheduled map with Equalize setting.
